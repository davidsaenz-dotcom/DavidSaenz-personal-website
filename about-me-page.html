A little bit about me...

My Passion

Ever since I was a little boy, I've been very curious about how things worked around me. I loved to fix stuff, dissassemble what I could, and figure out what made it tick. This is where my dream of becoming an enginner was born. In Ecuador, I started pursuing this dream by following a robotics course from a robotics school called Robotic Minds. That is where I started learning everything I needed to become an engineer one day. I started to learn programmmin on Arduinos and built bots for many different challenges. When we moved here to the US, I had the opportunity to further follow my dream by following the Computer Science diploma at my school. Another big reason why I want to become an engineer is my dad. He's an engineer as well and has engrained that itch to fix and automate fixes to problems. My parents both engrained that curiosity in me. This is why I am so sure that being an engineer is what I want to do.

A Brief History of Where I've Lived

My dad works in a company dedicated to providing energy technology and energy services to energy companies around the world. This is the reason why we've moved so much between countries. My parents had been moving even before I was born but I will start from my home country, Ecuador. Me, my parents, and my brother were all born in Ecuador. My first move happened when I was four years old. My dad's company had moved us to Houston for almost 2 years. We then moved back to Ecuador where we stayed for about a year before being moved to Colombia, Bogotá. We stayed there for almost 2 years again, when we then came back to Ecuador again. This time we stayed in Ecuador for 6 years before moving back to Houston from then to the present.
