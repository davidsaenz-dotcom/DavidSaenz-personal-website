My Passion

This is where my dream of becoming an enginner was born. In Ecuador, I started pursuing this dream by following a robotics course from a robotics school called Robotic Minds. That is where I started learning everything I needed to become an engineer one day. I started to learn programmmin on Arduinos and built bots for many different challenges. When we moved here to the US, I had the opportunity to further follow my dream by following the Computer Science diploma at my school. Another big reason why I want to become an engineer is my dad. He's an engineer as well and has engrained that itch to fix and automate fixes to problems. My parents both engrained that curiosity in me. This is why I am so sure that being an engineer is what I want to do.
